Part two

The part that matters most

Your events page was edited on 30 July 2026. Nine days before we looked at it. And Copper Bash 2023 was still on it after that edit.

That is the whole diagnosis in one fact. Somebody is in there maintaining the site. The problem is that the tool makes adding easy and removing invisible, and nothing in it knows that September 2023 has been and gone. So the new thing goes on top and the old thing stays underneath, and three years later a page you touched last week is still inviting people to a party that already happened.

You cannot fix that with more discipline. Here is the same page in your sitemap, which is how we know the dates:

/contact/ 24 September 2023
/menus/ 26 October 2023
/brunch/ 26 October 2023
/ 8 July 2025
/reserve/ 25 March 2026
/events/ 30 July 2026

Every date above comes straight from copperac.com/page-sitemap.xml ↗, which your own SEO plugin publishes. Menu and contact are the two pages a customer needs most, and they are the two nobody has been able to touch since 2023.
