Piece one · $750 + $59 a month

The site.

Your three priorities, in your order. This piece stands alone: everything in it works with the truck's window exactly as it runs today.

The schedule, always right

Where the truck is today and the rest of the week, big enough to read from a phone in a parking lot. You update it in one place; the site never shows yesterday. Facebook keeps doing what Facebook does, but the answer to the only question that matters stops depending on it.

Order ahead, from the schedule page

See where the truck is, tap the menu, pay, walk up when it is ready. Your kitchen side is one screen on your own phone: orders ring until you accept, a busy dial for slammed stops, a pause button, and an 86 board for when the carne asada runs out. Every steak taco, every salsa choice, and your food photos are already in it, pulled from your own menu. Ordering adds nothing to your monthly: a small fee on each online order, paid by the guest, is what keeps it that way.

Catering and events, booked while you cook

A booking form that asks the right questions (date, headcount, where) and lands in your inbox, so event requests stop living in Facebook comments and missed calls. The shirts sell on the site too, sizes and all.

Piece two · $249, once

The register.

This piece replaces what Toast does at your window, and the honest starting point is what you pay now. From your own numbers: $50 a month renting the wireless unit, plus 3.69% + 15¢ on every card tap, your own rate, which is the top of Toast's no-monthly pricing.

The replacement: a $249 Stripe reader you own outright, a big-button order screen with your 13 items on any tablet or phone, a cash button, and daily totals. No rental, no monthly, no other charge.

What we found.

The schedule lives in video captions

This week's stops are typed into Facebook video posts, one at a time, seen by whoever the feed picks. A customer who wants Tuesday's location has to go digging for it.

Online ordering exists and is switched off

The truck has a Toast ordering page, and when we checked it said currently not accepting online orders. The tool is there; it just is not working for you, and when it is on, it works on Toast's page rather than yours.

There is no website to find

Part four

The pieces, added up.

Piece one, the site: $750 to build, $59 a month. The monthly covers hosting, security, updates, and small content edits; it is care, not rent. Once the build is paid you own all of it: code, content, accounts, domain. Leave any time and take everything. Order-ahead is part of this piece and adds nothing monthly.

Piece two, the register: $249 once, whenever you want it, or never. It pays for itself out of the rent it ends, in about four months, and after that the dial above is money kept every month.

Together: $999 once and $59 a month, for the site, the schedule, catering, order-ahead, and a window that costs less to run than it does today.
